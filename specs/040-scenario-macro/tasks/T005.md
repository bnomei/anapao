## Context

Act as a fresh independent Sol/high validator after T001-T004. Do not accept implementation-worker
self-review or reduced smoke tests. First confirm `039-checked-scenario-authoring/T006` remains
`done` and verification-passed, then review the final macro against the complete predecessor
public API and every R001-R012 acceptance anchor.

Grammar review must enumerate—not sample—all 15 families, every native/typed config field, common
node/scenario/edge fields, every transfer, resource/state connection and target, tracking, every end
leaf/composite/pass-through, defaults, repeated ends, and all legal trailing separators. Confirm no
partial/MVP branch, undocumented accepted syntax, or field-order trap remains.

Expansion review must prove:

- only `$crate`-qualified public spec-039 types/constructors/setters and absolute standard paths;
- exact config `Default`/`with_*`/`without_*`, all family constructors, node common setters,
  resource/state constructors/setters, edge constructors/setters, and builder methods;
- no private field, raw validation, `ValidatedExpressions`, plan, engine, DTO tag/payload, or
  macro-only semantic hook;
- exact symbol IDs created once, separate namespaces, safe registry lookup, forward targets, and
  registry misses routed to unchanged checked-builder missing-reference diagnostics;
- every captured expression appears once in executable expansion;
- `Result<Scenario, SetupError>` with no panic/unwrap/expect/unreachable/index/fixture/unchecked
  extraction introduced by the macro;
- focused syntax errors and unchanged checked-builder semantic errors.

Compiler-boundary review must inspect every pass/fail source and `.stderr`, execute trybuild,
verify no-implicit-prelude/internal-name hygiene and one-evaluation pass execution, and run the real
offline Cargo dependency-rename fixture. Confirm `trybuild` is dev-only and the lockfile change is
narrow rather than a dependency refresh.

Runtime review must inspect full family/surface/error/counter coverage, then independently trace the
direct-builder and macro scenarios through checked equality, public compilation, identical seed/
config, and full `RunReport` equality. Existing checked-authoring behavior must remain green.

Docs/export review must prove one documented macro at root and prelude only, complete executable
examples, retained DTO/direct-builder routes, accurate grammar/error/evaluation/rename/SemVer
guidance, and explicit rejection of `expectations!`/assertion/config/report/procedural/helper macro
scope. Check MSRV 1.85 and edition 2021 compatibility.

You may make narrow corrections within task scope, rerun affected focused tests, and then rerun all
full gates. A grammar/API redesign, missing predecessor API, partial coverage, or extra macro must
be rejected back to its owning task rather than waved through.
